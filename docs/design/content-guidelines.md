# Content guidelines

> **What this is:** how BizzAgent writes and speaks, in all three languages.
> **Who reads it:** anyone writing UI copy, prompts or templates; translators.
> **Last reviewed:** 2026-10-02

## Voice

Plain, warm and specific. Never hype. BizzAgent sounds like a careful bookkeeper who is on your
side.

| Do | Don't |
|---|---|
| "Your gross margin is 32%. Here is how we got it." | "Great news! Your margins look amazing!" |
| "I don't have a verified source for that. Would you like an advisor?" | "Generally, in Ethiopia, you need to…" (uncited) |
| "Reading your licence…" | "Working on it…" |
| "We ask this because the funder scores job creation." | (no reason given) |
| "This is a draft. Nothing is sent until you approve." | "Submitted!" (when it wasn't) |

## Rules

1. **Say where numbers come from.** "From your March sales you told me about (unverified)."
2. **Name uncertainty.** Use the provenance words exactly: established, unverified, missing,
   contradictory.
3. **Short sentences.** One idea per sentence. Aim for a reading level a 12-year-old understands.
4. **Second person, active voice.** "You can…", "BizzAgent will…".
5. **No jargon without a TermTooltip.**
6. **"Not advice" notices** on legal, tax and investment screens. Use the exact reviewed
   wording, never paraphrased.
7. **Numbers.** Use digits, "Br" with a thin space, and dual dates.
8. **Errors.** Say what happened, why if known, and what to do. Never blame the user.
9. **Respectful register** in Amharic and Oromo. Use the formal "you" (እርስዎ) in Amharic UI.
   The Oromo register is to be confirmed by a native reviewer.
