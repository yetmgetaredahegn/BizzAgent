# ADR-0026: Document explainer: grounding, red-flag rules, legal-safety boundary

- **Status:** Accepted
- **Date:** 2026-10-02

## Context

Users photograph contracts and notices they don't understand.

## Decision

Every explanation point cites a span and page. Red flags come from a deterministic rule library. Legal context comes only from pack entries. Unreadable regions are reported.

## Consequences

Useful and safe. It never gives a legal opinion.
