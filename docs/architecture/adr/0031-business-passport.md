# ADR-0031: Business Passport and consented sharing

- **Status:** Accepted
- **Date:** 2026-10-02

## Context

Businesses re-enter the same facts for every funder and can't prove them.

## Decision

The Passport snapshots facts with evidence grades. Shares are scoped, expiring and revocable, and every view is audited.

## Consequences

Reusable verified profiles. Extra share-management UI.
