# ADR-0018: Safety boundaries for legal and financial guidance

- **Status:** Accepted
- **Date:** 2026-10-02

## Context

Users may act on what BizzAgent says about law, tax and money.

## Decision

Explain, don't decide. Legal and tax statements require a pack citation. Investment, tax and legal determinations are never made. Every such screen has a "not advice" notice and a professional pointer.

## Consequences

Lower liability and higher trust. Some questions get "no verified answer".
