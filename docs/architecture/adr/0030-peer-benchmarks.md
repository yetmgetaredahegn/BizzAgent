# ADR-0030: Peer benchmarks with opt-in and k-anonymity ≥ 10

- **Status:** Accepted
- **Date:** 2026-10-02

## Context

Benchmarks are valuable but can re-identify small businesses.

## Decision

Opt-in contribution, coarse bins, k ≥ 10 per cell, small cells hidden, no raw export.

## Consequences

Privacy-safe comparisons. Benchmarks appear only once enough businesses contribute.
