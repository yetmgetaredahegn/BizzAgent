# ADR-0032: Autonomy levels L0–L3 and the approvals inbox

- **Status:** Accepted
- **Date:** 2026-10-02

## Context

Agentic actions must stay under user control.

## Decision

Each skill and mission has a level. A pure policy decides allowed / needs_approval / forbidden. Consequential actions become inbox cards (voice approval with read-back). L3 hard exclusions: signing, legal submission, third-party payments, personal space.

## Consequences

Safe autonomy. More interaction for cautious users.
