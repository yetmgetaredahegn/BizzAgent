# ADR-0017: Rename FundFlow to BizzAgent and drop sequa

- **Status:** Accepted
- **Date:** 2026-10-02

## Context

The product grew from a single-funder hackathon tool into a general business agent.

## Decision

Rename to BizzAgent (package `bizzagent`, env prefix `BIZZAGENT_`), remove all sequa branding, and archive the hackathon brief.

## Consequences

A clear identity. Old links and storage keys are invalidated.
