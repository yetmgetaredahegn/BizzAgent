# Business glossary: English · Amharic · Afaan Oromo

> **What this is:** the canonical terms used in UI copy, TermTooltips and prompts.
> **Who reads it:** translators, content reviewers, engineers writing prompts.
> **Last reviewed:** 2026-10-02

> **Every Amharic and Afaan Oromo term below is a DRAFT pending native-speaker review.** Terms
> marked `TODO(native review)` have no draft yet. Legal-form names must match the wording in
> official sources, which are cited in the country pack, before release.
